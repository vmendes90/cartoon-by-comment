Questions

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Claude writes the code. You give notes and make decisions. The book shows what to ask at each step and how to check that the result is right.

Do I need a paid Claude plan?

Claude Code needs Claude Pro or higher. Chapter 07 also shows a manual route on the free plan: slower, but it works for a first test.

Windows or Mac?

Both. The starter was tested on Windows 11, and the Mac commands are included.

Can I make a series in another language?

Yes. The examples are in English and the method works in any language. Some of the What-if and 3D examples were made for a Portuguese-speaking audience.

Can I monetise the videos I make?

Yes, they are yours. The book explains the AI labels each platform asks for and which free voices and sounds you can use commercially.

Is this a get-rich course?

No. It is a production method with real files and real mistakes. It does not promise views, followers or income.

How long does an episode take?

Setup takes an evening. Your first episode takes a few days, because you design the character and the world. Later episodes reuse the character, the sets and the tools, so they go faster.

What does it cost per month?

Claude Pro is $20 a month and runs Claude Code. A natural paid voice is optional (ElevenLabs Starter, $6 a month); your own voice or the computer's voice is free. The renderer and the sounds in the book are free.

I have never used a terminal. Can I do this?

Yes. The book shows the few commands you need, and Claude Code can run the setup for you: you paste one prompt and approve each step.

Why not just use an AI video tool?

Video generators redraw the character every clip, so it drifts, and every clip costs credits. Here Claude writes code that draws the character, so it stays the same every day, every frame can be fixed, and rendering is free on your own computer.

What if nobody votes?

The book has a section for that: easy one-word questions, your own pinned vote comment, and an honest way to decide on screen when only a few people vote.
